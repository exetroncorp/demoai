MISSIONS

Drive into a cyan beacon to start a job. A floating arrow above you and the M-map briefing always point to your next step. Hot Wheels = steal & deliver a car, Checkpoint Rush = hit 5 cyan gates in any vehicle, JAVA Gang War = hunt the crew in red JAVA shirts. Beat the timer for bonus cash.

WANTED STARS

Crimes raise your stars and spawn police. Break line of sight and stay 55m+ away to fill the evade bar and lose a star. Getting WASTED resets them but costs progress.

GETTING AROUND

Steal parked or moving cars, grab the Sparrow heli at the BNPP campus esplanade (SPACE lift · W/S pitch · A/D turn), or just sprint. Health regenerates when out of combat; red cross pickups heal instantly.
