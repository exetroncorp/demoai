MISSIONS

Drive into a cyan beacon to start a job — eight rotate randomly: Hot Wheels steal & deliver a car, Checkpoint Rush hit 5 cyan gates, JAVA Gang War hunt the crew in red JAVA shirts, Scrap Yard Royale wreck 3 gang runners, Courier Rush grab 6 glowing parcels, Heat Run survive the police chase… and Dog Taxi: steal the DOGMOBILE convertible and chauffeur its canine crew to the drop-off — but never shoot a dog, the pack remembers. The MJ crew's vintage boombox spins anything you drop in /musics/jacksons, and thobe-and-keffiyeh street cats debke-step through Old Town. And if a Grassy Knuckle or Hit on the Don beacon pops up: grab the helicopter, climb to the rooftop nest, and snipe the gang boss waving from his black convertible as the police-escorted motorcade rolls past. Beat the timer for bonus cash.

WANTED STARS

Crimes raise your stars and spawn police. Break line of sight and stay 55m+ away to fill the evade bar and lose a star. Getting WASTED resets them but costs progress.

GETTING AROUND

Steal parked or moving cars, grab the Sparrow heli at the BNPP campus esplanade (SPACE lift · W/S pitch · A/D turn), or just sprint. Health regenerates when out of combat; red cross pickups heal instantly.

WEATHER & HEAT

Montreuil rolls through clear, cloudy, rain and thunderstorm cycles. Wet asphalt means less grip — brake early and expect slides. Pedestrians pop umbrellas, street lights flicker on, thunder shakes the sky — and at 3+ wanted stars a police helicopter joins the chase with a searchlight. Your cash, gym gains and rockets auto-save.
